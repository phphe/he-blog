Record an unexpected bug.

This error means that the entry file of the package, such as "index.js", cannot be found, which is defined by the `main` and `module` properties of `package.json`.

But I went in the wrong direction at the beginning. My project is a `monorepo`, using pnpm workspace, and recently used the "workspace:xxx" protocol of pnpm. So I suspect that vite does not recognize the workspace protocol and cannot find the package. I tried to upgrade vite, but it didn't work.

What was the final reason? Because I re-cloned the project, the package folder (dist) of the sub-project was gone, so vite could naturally find the library but not the entry file. Just re-execute the `build` command.
